      Help

      Getting a good photo

      • Stand straight in front of the board, not at an angle.
      • Fill the screen with the part you want to read.
      • Avoid glare from windows. Hold the phone still.
      • Clear, neat printing works best. Joined handwriting may need fixing.

      Reading tools

      • OpenDyslexic font has heavy letter bottoms so b and d do not flip.
      • Syllables splits long words into small parts: ma·ngwa·na·ni.
      • Change the background colour until reading feels comfortable.
      • Listen reads the text aloud and highlights each sentence.

      Privacy

      Everything happens on this phone. Photos and notes are never uploaded.
